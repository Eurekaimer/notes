## Brief History

How does visual intelligence work? That's the core of the course. If you know the principles of human vision, you can pass. Introduce the principles of vision (Nobel Prize), and the first Ph.D. paper in CV, btw, and more history.

How to recover information from 2D to 3D is the core question. You should use some validation tools (like triangle methods). And our language is 1D (a generated "thing"), but the real world is not 1D, so language and vision are very different (CV and NLP).

AI winter (the methods and works didn't deliver) came.

The breakthrough happened in the 1980s with FNN's Backprop! (Hinton and LeCun) And we get through DL in the 2020s. Then ImageNet appears.
